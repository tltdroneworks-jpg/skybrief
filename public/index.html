Not an official briefing. This site is a convenience pack. It is not an official South African meteorological or NOTAM briefing and must not be the sole source for a flight decision. Official MET is SAWS. Official NOTAMs, PIBs and flight-plan filing are from ATNS File2Fly (free registration). Airspace from the ATNS RSA DATA KMZ is a gross representation only and is not for navigation. The drone overlay is a planning aid, not permission to fly.
